Give Gem Dust and five plain Apotheosis salvage materials EMC with ProjectE. Once you obtain them, you can transmute more for reforging and gem crafting. The five materials are Mysterious Scrap Metal, Timeworn Fabric, Luminous Crystal Shard, Arcane Sands, and Godforged Pearl.

For Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge with Apotheosis 8.9.0 and ProjectE 1.1.0. Apotheosis's required modules are also needed. Gems, affix gear, socketed equipment, and other items carrying unique state are excluded. The EMC prices are set by this addon; they are not pre-existing ProjectE values. This addon does not alter recipes, loot, or item behavior.

An isolated server confirmed all six mapped prices with ProjectE Integration and Recipe Integration present. Client Transmutation Table output-taking, learned-item persistence, and individual modpack overrides have not been checked.
